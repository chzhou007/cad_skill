# Historical view candidates and current ownership

Before reporting main/detail candidates as open HIL questions, run
`scripts/review_view_lineage.py` against the current graph and original review
data. First run the existing review-data preflight to verify original Pair files.
Pin the current graph and source SHA256. The topology owner's independent CAD
replay remains a separate prerequisite; this helper does not repeat it.

Follow the old abbreviated edge through `confirmedSideviewCanonicalization`.
Require the original received answer and Pair hash, preserved retired snapshots,
current canonical edge, detail T and matching service. Check equipment ownership
against current endpoints and source identity. A changed answer, missing alias,
duplicate representation or stale owner blocks the claimed disposition.
An explicitly reopened original question instead invalidates its old relation
and returns a reconciliation status; keep the review page available and retain
the old answer as history. Do not silently keep its original confirmation.

Keep three facts separate:

- The main/detail or sideview relation has been confirmed.
- The current canonical interface belongs to an identified equipment instance.
- The complete main network, physical ports and installed length are proved.

A confirmed sideview can have an unresolved equipment owner. Neither fact
proves projected main identity or physical length. Keep unknowns explicit and
retain the original questions and answers as history; do not automatically
confirm a rebuilt graph or generate repeat questions.
