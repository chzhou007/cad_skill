# Axis DN inheritance within one unbranched native source path

Use `reconstruct-axis-unbranched-vertical-DN` after the original segment-DN and
direction owners. Independently replay `validate-axis-unbranched-vertical-DN`.
Re-read their original controls and native bindings before using a DN seed.
This fills vertical portions only, including same-floor rises/drops, from
drawing 11. Horizontal geometry can prove continuity without supplying a
horizontal attribute value; horizontal DN/length still belongs to matched plans.

Screen every currently unknown vertical domain. Preserve old axis-riser and
annotation domains separately. An original ambiguous/unparsed annotation remains
blocked. A qualifying seed and target must occupy the same original edge and
attribute path. Do not join separate paths because their edge ID matches.

Reconstruct every native LINE child in the intervening corridor. Exact XYZ
contacts must have exactly two same-system source arms, including native
primitives outside the selected graph path. A third arm, duplicate LINE,
unwitnessed span, inline-symbol gap or source-Z mismatch stops this rule. It
does not replace the existing true-T inheritance owner or create new nodes.
Collinear subdivision of one native LINE is a two-arm continuation, not a T.

Keep the original annotation owner, text, child/root pose and source evidence.
Reject different seed values, any unowned in-scope DN (even the same number),
compound/unsupported captions, nested DN without its original owner, an
intersecting ordered F stem or an F-owned seed. Do not choose the most common
number or widen a nearest-text distance. The old F and repeated-layout readers
remain authoritative for their scopes and require their independent controls.

New records reference original edge/path/segment IDs, the original seed record,
the native bend/ray chain and annotation barriers. They assign no whole-edge DN,
physical elevation or length. Source Z proves contact only. Preserve all old
plan attributes, source geometry, component identities and annotations. Source
qualification, actual viewer/CSV publication and human acceptance are separate.
Cover reflected/rotated branches, ordinary crossings, real third arms,
duplicate source lines, different Z, new DN/F barriers and separate-path gaps in
regression before distributing the rule.
