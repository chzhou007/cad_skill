After confirmed main/detail aliases have native member interfaces, screen every
external port with `reconstruct-alias-member-inline-bridges` and independently
replay `validate-alias-member-inline-bridges --bridge-run RUN`.

An assigned soft joint can exist in the original component inventory while a
long, bent drawing edge lacks `source_flexible_joints`. This is an owner-entry
gap, not evidence that the pipe is disconnected. Reuse the existing native
joint reader on the unique original assigned source instance and calibrated
block. Establish its transformed POINT/bar contacts and outward same-system
native LINE rays before deriving metadata in a private reading domain.

Two independent member endpoints bound that domain. Clip only collinear
original drawing fragments to the interval; retain real gaps for the original
pipe/joint continuity owner to check. Do not add a straight line across a gap.
Keep unrelated drawing tails, source paths, nodes and all component assignments
unchanged. The private projection has its own hash; it is not a replacement
local topology. A component outside the bounded scope, competing carrier,
namespace collision, source XYZ mismatch or duplicate native LINE blocks it.

The unchanged member bridge owner must still prove reciprocal axis ports,
original three-ray Ts, a pure horizontal axis path, absence of interior circles,
ordered actual valve/joint identities and independent plan-member DN agreement.
DN never chooses a mapping. Single-ended ports remain explicit pending routes.
Do not loosen those requirements to make a larger cohort pass.

Rebuild the separate member graph before composing attributes. A qualified
bridge exposes plan DN and actual pipe-only projection; inline graphic spans
do not supply physical soft-joint length, complete centreline, installed length
or cut length. All vertical attributes remain on drawing 11. Source qualification,
viewer publication, provider inventory inheritance and HIL are separate stages.
Run historic inline/member, rotated/mirrored joint, wrong-system/duplicate-line,
private-domain clipping and pipeline tests before publishing a new asset version.
