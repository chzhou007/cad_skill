# Named lower-floor correspondence on mixed-edge prefixes

Use `match_named_riser_lower_floor_segments.py` when an existing mixed axis
edge begins at a lower-floor network T, follows an in-plane approach and ends
with one independently checked cross-floor vertical segment. Its existing
network-to-device bridge must reach an independently checked service T in both
views. Require both services of the same source-ready equipment and floor.

Reproduce the native plan RIF naming and the selected main-view axis labels.
The axis label's literal native target must include the exact source LINE of
one verified named column. The selected lower vertical segment must belong to
that column, span the declared floor pair and end at its already checked middle
T. A nearby repeated detail label, same DN, shaft name or projected position
cannot choose a column. Native source coverage and the inherited full-column
verifier remain required.

The lower approach is the original prefix before that vertical segment.
Preserve its original indices and exact points, and reject another plumb
stroke in the selected prefix. At both network/device Ts, reuse the inherited
native opposing-ray and XYZ checks; the approach must be the network third
arm and the bridge part of both through mains. Recheck source coverage on a
private prefix projection. Plan paths reuse the existing exact-touching
reader; genuine gaps do not become new pipe spans.

Output two segment records per service: lower approach and existing bridge.
The bend is an attribute limit, not a new physical node. Both local graphs,
vertical scopes, supply/return isolation and unknown physical ports/lengths
remain unchanged. Naming is identity evidence, not horizontal DN inheritance
or physical length.

Run `pipeline.py validate-lower-floor-segments --mapping-run ...` for independent
native replay. It shares the existing segment-mapping gate while dispatching
to the lower-prefix owner. Success stays pending/exit 2 for segment-aware plan
attributes, remaining interfaces, source comparison and HIL. A whole-edge or
upper-suffix consumer cannot read these records as certified values.

Run `test_match_named_riser_lower_floor_segments.py`,
`test_lower_floor_segment_mapping_gate.py`, the inherited upper mapper and
native naming, T/XYZ, column, path, wave/crossing and metric regressions.
SH16 08/11 CHWS2/CHWR2 and CRAH-4#-1-8 form one scoped case; they do not prove
all first-floor connections or cross-floor lengths.
