# Reapply mature readers after plan equipment ownership changes

A repaired equipment endpoint changes the available branch and main anchors.
Do not stop at a repaired plan graph or retain a discovery-only mapping queue.
`rebuild_ordered_owner_crossview.py` replays the logical-owner parent and native
axis caption identity, reuses native branch/T/path readers in both drawings,
then batches existing correspondence discovery over the floor. Its manifest
pins both local graphs, native CAD, inventories, old batch, member policy,
confirmed compound-lane reply and dimension-corroborated plan units.

Old correspondences retain their original IDs, native evidence and graph
versions; current service traces must still agree. Do not replace their IDs
with the ordinal `BATCH-F…` IDs returned by a fresh discovery pass. New IDs use
kind, floor, system and original two-view path identities. A missing old path,
parallel writer or competing axis owner blocks this extension. Old evidence is
historical, not described as freshly reconstructed by a scoped extension.

For newly available members, reuse `confirmed_member_plan_paths.collect` and
its original projection, wave, detail-T and unit readers. Independently replay
the old member owner and require every old member record to remain exact.
Expanded groups require the same complete, independently confirmed aliases;
neither DN nor equipment-number order establishes a new mapping. Formal entry:
`reconstruct-ordered-owner-crossview --manifest … --output NEW`, followed by
`validate-ordered-owner-crossview --mapping-run NEW`.

`ordered_owner_plan_context.py` and `refresh_ordered_owner_plan_attributes.py`
publish only newly proved one-to-one horizontal T-to-T mains on a qualified
axis artifact. Independent native mappings may also describe equipment branches;
their detail-drawing spans or unknown connectors do not become physical length.
Keep plan, axis and fused contexts versioned separately. Mixed/vertical segments
continue to use the existing segment-direction rule and axis-native attributes.

Preserve all old effective UI/CSV values, component identities, annotation
history and every vertical domain. New centreline is the plan T-to-T run,
including inline assembly extents; installed and net cut lengths remain unknown.
The new mapping context changes review identity without rewriting either local
topology or inheriting HIL. Use `validate-ordered-owner-plan-refresh --bundle …`,
native wide/local source pairs and actual browser/CSV checks before publishing.

Required negatives: lost or duplicated old paths, discovery reordering, writer
collision, source/hash changes, equal counts with wrong instances, same-floor
vertical or mixed targets, changed old member values, and installed/net length
fabrication. Full-floor structural matching remains a separate gate; scoped
native extensions do not make a previously unresolved full-floor match pass.
