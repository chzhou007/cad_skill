# Complete native rows resolve competing equipment projection strips

Use this rule only after original main/detail aliases were confirmed and the
local topology owner independently replays. A valid individual projection strip
can still be insufficient: shifted terminal pairs may both lie under one body,
leaving its neighbouring equipment falsely unconnected.

Reuse the original native body, caption, paired-circle/four-line glyph and
detail-T readers. Screen the entire confirmed pair cohort and every equipment
body on that native row. Confirmed aliases preserve the relationship to detail
branches. Native source circle population must exactly equal the complete pair
population; missing/extra glyphs or bodies block the rule.

Require at least three independently established old owner pairs. Re-read their
native projection and both current detail Ts. Sort pairs and bodies by their
native row coordinates, never by label numbers, DN, nearest body or a requested
target list. Equal cardinality plus a unique monotone bijection must agree with
every independent owner. Preserve contradictory controls as a failure; do not
reassign them to make the row fit. This is a calibrated drawing convention,
not proof of a physical equipment nozzle or an unshown connector length.

Apply the unresolved pairs to the canonical detail branches, preserving the
old alias/metric coordinate domain and recording the current extension input
hash separately. Keep every old owner record, source path, component instance
and equipment. Archive resolved interfaces rather than deleting their evidence.
Retain supply/return roles and explicit unknown physical lengths.

Run `pipeline.py reconstruct-ordered-sideview-owners --manifest ... --output ...`
for production and independent native validation; `validate-ordered-sideview-owners`
replays it separately. Original unmodified local owners are freshly verified,
or reused only within the same job after checking their observed dependencies.
Source overview/local/main-detail comparisons and inherited regressions precede
publication. Updated local connectivity invalidates affected mappings, DN and
length fusion; an old axis result does not automatically gain new attributes.

SH16 regression: 18 native pairs and 18 bodies, 16 independent old owners, two
shifted pairs. L1 binds equipment 2-7, L2 binds 2-8. Four canonical branches gain
logical owners; no source pipe, component, hydraulic parameter or length changes.
