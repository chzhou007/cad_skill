# Batch existing plan metrics; scope ancestor joint holds to actual children

Run `reconstruct-anchored-plan-metrics` and `validate-anchored-plan-metrics`
against the complete current provider. The entry replays its original owner in
a pinned runtime, screens every horizontal scope still lacking complete length,
and requires a unique whole-path ordinary correspondence. Member, partial,
detail, explicit source-discrepancy and real-gap scopes retain their owners.

Reuse original native T/path, exact touching-subdivision, unit-dimension and
centreline readers. A DN conflict does not prevent an independently verified
length; it still prevents selecting DN. All axis vertical portions, including
same-floor rises, keep their 11-source ownership. No installed or net-cut length
is inferred from drawn geometry.

After splitting an old repaired edge, its coarse soft-joint hold can incorrectly
survive on every child. Do not clear it merely because the child has no joint
field or because a drawn LINE is continuous. Replay the complete original joint
origin population, calibrated block fingerprint, transformed POINT/bar contacts,
native pipe rays and unique original inventory ownership. Compare finite native
joint contact intervals with the child's exact source paths, including mirrored
or rotated cases. A relevant joint remains unknown; missing origins, another
reason or an untracked child keeps the hold. Ordinary orthogonal crossings do
not identify a joint or connect different systems.

When every origin lies outside the checked child, retain the old metadata and
evidence as history and add a separately qualified complete-centreline scope.
Replay both native endpoint Ts and ordered complete component signatures in
both views. Do not relocate or duplicate components. Preserve all original DN,
vertical, topology, stable-ID and annotation payloads.

`refresh_anchored_native_plan_metrics.refresh` and
`validate-anchored-native-metric-viewer` adopt only the qualified metrics. They
enable `edge-attributes-anchored-metrics.js` as an explicit versioned facade;
the original `edge-attributes.js` bytes and its default export contract remain
unchanged. Append metric CSV fields, preserve the old columns/values, retain original DN
presentation and expose the current source range in Chinese. Rebuild the full
provider/frontier from the actual rendered values, leaving correspondence and
member namespaces unchanged. Verify actual UI and downloads before reporting
the updated counts. Technical scope completion does not imply HIL acceptance.
