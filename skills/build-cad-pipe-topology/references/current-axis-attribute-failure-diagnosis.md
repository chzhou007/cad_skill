# Diagnose attribute failures before another correspondence or image review

Run `diagnose-axis-attribute-failures` against the pinned current source, local
topology, routing, complete provider and prior DN evidence. Separate current
hash checking from fresh native replay. The diagnostic re-reads floor/elevation
glyphs, while prior DN evidence is hash checked and explicitly not presented as
a new reconstruction or independent acceptance.

Distinguish these failures before changing rules:

- A horizontal edge without correspondence needs topology matching; an already
  mapped edge with a DN conflict or unknown inline interior does not.
- A vertical segment without its own caption may have a seed on another path
  of the same logical edge, or on a source-connected main at a true T. The old
  bend-only reader stops at path boundaries and physical Ts. Reuse the original
  omission/inline and T source owners to compose those scopes before extending
  inheritance. Do not use nearest labels, row order or unrelated plan DN.
- Repeated native caption ownership, ambiguous text projection and missing
  native LINE coverage are distinct failures. Keep their original reasons;
  do not hide them behind an undifferentiated unknown-node count.
- Elevation labels may be native TEXT plus a three-stroke elevation glyph and
  baseline, even when the drawing has no native DIMENSION objects. Read Chinese
  floor text and the numeric caption together with their original handles.

`axis_floor_elevation_texts.collect` supports the native modelspace TEXT/LINE
glyph, including horizontal reflection. Competing numbers or baselines remain
held. Its raw elevation values do not select an engineering unit or establish a
pipe centre elevation. Floor separation alone does not measure a same-floor
drop or a vertical pipe whose endpoints have different relative heights.

Length adoption therefore requires both pipe endpoints, their floor/elevation
references and units, plus pipe-centre offsets or a directly owned source
dimension. Never convert graphic Y distance, source Z=0, parenthesized DN suffix
or a floor-height difference into a pipe length without that evidence.

Every vertical portion uses drawing 11, including same-floor rises/drops.
Horizontal source values use the matched floor plan. After a common rule is
extended, batch the complete affected cohort, preserve old values and sources,
and invalidate/recompute dependent scopes. Diagnostic counts and candidate
seeds are not newly adopted pipe attributes or HIL acceptance.
