# Circle-bracketed compound member interfaces

Use this rule when a source-qualified lower-member branch cohort lies on a
compound plan LINE, isolated native circles bracket its first/last branch, and
the axis view independently shows the approaches and primary main. It inherits
the expert-confirmed overlap convention; it does not turn an arbitrary circle
or ordinary orthogonal crossing into a T.

`native_paired_caption_geometry` reserves F frames before bare captions,
including pending F frames and exploded frames with extra crossings. It retains
the original end-column reader. Its new interior family requires two same-profile
native TEXT captions straddling exactly two parallel service LINEs, unique
one-to-one order, interior stations and conservative nominal-height clearance.
DN and literal level values never select a pipe. A third pipe, duplicate LINE,
unsupported transform or competing pair stays unresolved. Repeated captions on
one LINE must agree before selecting a member.

`compound_member_interfaces` requires the entire nonduplicated inherited
lower-member chain, at least two independently identified equipment branches,
monotone branch order in both views and unique nearest outer circle brackets.
An interior/duplicate circle or missing bracket blocks the cohort. Every axis
approach, primary main and primary T is reread from native geometry. Circle
centres locate interfaces; radii are never lengths.

The axis caption's literal level selects one plan declaration, then its DN is
only a cross-check. Rebuild separate primary-member nodes and edges before
composing values. Lower approaches inherit the already qualified plan member DN;
a new/conflicting/ambiguous direct or F-owned annotation stops inheritance.
Unsupported TEXT, nearby MTEXT and nested DN require their original readers.
The guarded end-column reread must retain every inherited member caption.

Primary T-to-T centreline uses the original native plan unit/length reader and
undirected axis horizontal-domain predicate. It includes inline assembly
extents, not net pipe cut or installed whole length. Approaches containing a
local rise have only a known plan projection; do not subtract literal levels
to manufacture a rise length or assign the projected length to the whole edge.
Source Z is not physical elevation.

Keep each primary T's third source port, even when its external member route is
not yet qualified. Preserve original drawing/axis graphs, equipment identities
and axis component ownership. Plan component-to-member allocation and global
calculation readiness remain separate. Do not attach every projected valve to
both members or delete external ports to make the scope look complete.

Run `rebuild_compound_member_interfaces.py --manifest MANIFEST --output NEW`,
then `pipeline.py validate-compound-member-interfaces --interface-run NEW`.
The manifest pins all native inputs and an immutable original parent version.
`checked_native_parent_application` runs its content-checked artifact entry and
compares complete original proof bytes on every call. Edited inherited algorithm
bytes require parent requalification; independent new files may extend them.
Report parent artifact reuse separately from the new family's native reread.

Use the versioned `refresh_compound_interface_attributes.py` and
`validate-compound-interface-attribute-refresh --bundle VIEWER`. Require the
original independently qualified parent artifact and unchanged native inputs;
preserve its entire context and all old effective values. New values must pass
actual browser/CSV comparison and wide/local source Pairs before publication.
Bundle the rebuilt scoped member graph as a separate payload. Keep annotation
history and invalidate effective-value HIL. Technical scope success does not
accept unresolved external routes, the complete plan or the whole project.
