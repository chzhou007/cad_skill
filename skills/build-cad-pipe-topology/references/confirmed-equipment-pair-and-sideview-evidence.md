# Inherited explicit pair and sideview evidence

These adapters inherit existing confirmed features. They are not newly inferred connections.

## Explicit equipment-pair circle rays

`reconstruct_confirmed_equipment_pairs.py` already consumes the original native source, reviewed graph, Pair image, reply and bounded policy. Its plan output has `source_confirmed_equipment_pair_circle` on the T and branch, `confirmedEquipmentPairReconstruction` on the graph, and the existing logical equipment attachment. Preserve that producer and independently replay the plan ancestry before invoking the ray reader.

`native_confirmed_equipment_pair_circle_evidence.collect` checks matching records, question/answer and attachment bindings, native equipment/caption, original source line XYZ, actual circle layer and offset, current owner/floor, three same-system incident edges and the recorded main splits. The two main rays touch the topology anchor; the branch ray starts at its native circle rim. Wrong circle colours and inconsistent source Z remain supported only within the replayed explicit confirmation. They are never generalized as a global colour or flattening rule. Unknown ports and omitted connector lengths remain unknown.

The inventory dispatches this schema before the ordinary center-touch T checker. Its earlier omission made the 16 already confirmed branches of SH16 09 devices 17–24 appear technically unsupported. The adapter changes evidence interpretation, not local graph connectivity or quantities.

Required negatives include changed source/confirmation/owner, duplicate or mixed rays, four-way incidence, changed native Z, an unrecorded later main split, protected non-node boundaries, and a physical port or length claim. The existing ordinary-T, crossing, F and source-reviewed circle rules remain applicable.

It calls the producer, independently replays it, then writes `reports/source-sideview-equipment-ownership-stage.json`. The source-comparison gate dispatches this layer before inherited ownership and checks the original ancestor report without rebinding its hash.
